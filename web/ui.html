      Local availability (bridge work only)
      
    
      This governs one thing: the local job-board search (the optional German Bundesagentur source) - retail,
      shift work, anything that needs you physically present. It has no effect on remote roles,
      teaching applications, or freelance work; none of those care about a postal code or a
      shift pattern. Change the radius here and the very next "Check for new roles" run uses it -
      it used to be frozen at whatever a discovery happened to write down once, with no way to
      change it short of a code edit.
      
    
      
      
      
      
      
      
      
    
      
    
      
      
      
      
      
    
      
    
    
      
  
      

  
      
    
      Scoring
      
    
      Every posting you save is scored automatically, 0-100, for how well your
      career data covers what it asks. Open a card to see the breakdown line by line.
      
    
        
      
      • Requirements, 60%: each requirement line in the posting, against the CV the
        Build panel would make. On that CV counts fully, in your data but not on the CV counts
        0.6 (add it), a gap counts nothing. Nice-to-haves count half; personal qualities are
        not counted.
        • 
      
      • Lead clarity, 15%: how clearly one of your threads owns the posting.
        • 
      
      • People or systems, 10%: for enablement and teaching roles only, whether the job
        enables people or configures systems (the title trap).
        • 
      
      • Evidence, 15%: a portfolio piece for that thread, ready to show or not.
        Not counted for service roles.
        • 
    
      
    
      A part that cannot be judged is left out and its weight shared, rather
      than guessed. Contract, relocation or German requirements are shown as "check first",
      not scored. A /fit review, or a number you type into a card, overrides the app's score.
      The thresholds below set the colours and what Today calls stale.
